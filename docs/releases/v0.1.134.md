# Stellar Secretary v0.1.134

- Archive a file or image directly from desktop Library Chat by leaving the message blank. The **Save settings** button stays visible at the bottom of the settings window while you scroll.
- In the updated iPhone companion, select several photos in Chat or Save, add more in another pass, and remove individual attachments. A single submission keeps up to eight attachments together, with a 10 MB total limit. Batch transfer requires the updated desktop and iPhone apps.
- In iPhone Chat you can take a photo from the attachment menu and dismiss the keyboard with **Done**. The app distinguishes uploading, waiting for the previous reply, and waiting for a connection.
- From an iPhone original-file preview, use **Save to Files** for any attachment or **Save to Photos** for an image. The app asks before saving the same image to Photos again.

## 中文

- 电脑端在「知识库对话」中只添加文件或图片、留空文字，即可直接归档。「保存设置」固定在设置窗口底部，滚动时仍可点击。
- 更新后的 iPhone 配套版在「聊天」和「归档」中支持一次选多张、分次追加和逐个移除。每次可一起发送最多 8 个附件，合计 10 MB。批量传输需要手机和电脑端都更新。
- iPhone 聊天附件菜单加入「拍照」，键盘可通过「完成」收起。待发状态会分别显示上传中、等待上一条回复或等待连接。
- iPhone 原件预览可将附件「存储到文件」，图片还可以「保存到照片」。同一张图再次通过 App 保存到相册时会先确认。
