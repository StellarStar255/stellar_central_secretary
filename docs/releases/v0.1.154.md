# Stellar Secretary v0.1.154

Timetable summaries are easier to scan. New image summaries group schedules by day and keep times, locations and exceptions beside the relevant event. The desktop reader also groups recognizable weekday entries in existing Chinese summaries without regenerating them.

Long image interpretations are collapsed by default. Expand one to check the visual details alongside the summary.

## 中文

课表和日程截图的摘要现在按天列出安排，时间、地点及“非本周”等说明紧跟对应事项。已有中文摘要中能识别的星期条目也会自动分组，打开即可阅读，无需重新整理。

较长的图片解读默认折叠，需要核对原图细节时可以展开。
