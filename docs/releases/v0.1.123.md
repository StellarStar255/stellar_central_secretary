# Stellar Secretary v0.1.123

Open original material directly from Library results.

- Single-click a result to show its side preview. Double-click to open the original link, PDF, image or video. Supported documents such as Word and Excel open in the system's default app. Notes and grouped records open in full details.
- The desktop now checks model consent for phone-originated questions. Authorization is scoped to the phone and model destination and can be withdrawn. Phone uploads are archived without automatic AI summarization and can be summarized manually on the desktop.
- The iPhone companion source now includes a local demo and privacy controls. This release provides desktop installers. The iPhone app remains available for development testing from source.

中文：知识库搜索结果支持快速双击打开原网页、PDF、图片、视频或系统默认程序可打开的文件。单击仍显示右侧预览，笔记和组合资料双击进入完整详情。桌面端为手机问答校验模型授权，手机上传只归档，可在电脑上手动整理。iPhone 源码新增演示和隐私控制，本次发布提供桌面安装包。
