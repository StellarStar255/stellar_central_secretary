# Stellar Secretary v0.1.163

In Auto-detect mode, entering a Chinese birthday note with a date saves the original text to the library, even after a question. Lunar dates keep their original wording. You can still ask when someone's birthday is or choose Library chat to discuss the date.

## 中文

在「自动识别」模式下，即使刚刚问过问题，输入「小林生日 农历八月十二」这样的备注也会直接归档，保留原文和农历日期。输入「小林的生日是哪天？」仍会检索知识库，也可以选择「知识库对话」继续讨论这个日期。
