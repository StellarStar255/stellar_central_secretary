# Stellar Secretary v0.1.89

Keep clarifications and corrections in the conversation instead of silently archiving them as notes.

- **Persistent conversation intent:** after a chat starts answering questions, ordinary text in Auto-detect continues that conversation, including declarative clarifications, ownership confirmations and corrections. Follow-ups no longer depend on a fixed list of question phrases. Intent survives reloads, failed answers and intermediate accidental archives in older conversations.
- **Explicit archive controls:** save instructions, commands and explicit archive modes retain their existing behavior. Archive-only and note modes display a clear “saved without an answer” notice and a **Back to conversation** button that preserves the unsent message. Selecting a question mode clears Archive-only; a new chat clears Archive-only and stale archive modes.
- **Use the user's stated basis:** when a user confirms whose timetable it is or asks to judge from that timetable, continue answering the original question. Give a conditional conclusion with the visible time range, date differences and unverified assumptions rather than repeatedly requesting the same material. User assumptions are distinguished from facts established by the archived source.
- Chinese and English mode hints, placeholders and controls are updated consistently.

Validation: 336 unit tests with 10 platform-specific/opt-in skips, chat UI regression, real-browser clarification/legacy-conversation recovery/reload/archive-warning checks, company-data confirmation, Archive-only/background-summary and Chinese/English checks passed. A configured real model rechecked the original image over three conversational turns in an isolated copy, including the declarative confirmation; original records were unchanged. No private images or test conversations are shipped.

中文：本版把自动模式改为持续对话：一旦开始问答，同一会话里的普通补充、纠正、对象归属确认都继续回答，不再依赖固定追问句式。刷新、回答失败或旧对话中间误归档过消息，也不会清空对话意图。明确保存指令、命令及显式归档模式仍可保存资料。

「仅归档」和笔记模式会明确提示“只保存，不会回答”，并提供保留草稿的「切回对话」按钮。切换提问模式会关闭仅归档；新对话清除仅归档和遗留归档模式，避免设置误带入。

用户确认“这张课表就是她的，依据这个判断”时，会承接原问题，按指定依据给出注明前提、图示时间范围及日期差异的条件性结论，不把假设冒充已经核实的本周安排，也不反复索要同一份资料。

已通过完整单元测试、声明式补充和旧对话恢复的浏览器回归、公司资料确认、仅归档及中英文检查；另以真实模型在隔离副本中完成三轮原图复核，原资料未被修改。
